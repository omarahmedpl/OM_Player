OM Player — home screen widgets

Artwork leads, one rose accent, everything else stays quiet. The widget reads as a piece of the app, not a coloured tile: same 22 dp corner as the app's cards, same rose as its primary action.

Sizes to ship

Small · 2×2

Cover, title, artist, and a play/pause badge. Tap the cover opens the player; the badge toggles playback. For people who want the music visible but not the controls.

Wide · 4×2

The main one. Cover + track + previous / play / next, with a 2 dp progress hairline pinned to the bottom edge. Every control is a 44 dp target.

Compact · 4×1

One line for dense home screens. Optional — say if you want it, otherwise I skip it and keep the build smaller.

Why these choices

  • Artwork is the hero. The scrim only darkens the bottom third, so covers stay recognisable instead of being washed out by a gradient.
  • One accent, one place. Rose is only the play button and the progress line. Everything else is neutral, so the widget never competes with the wallpaper.
  • Honest surfaces. Android widgets can't blur, so the prototype uses the solid surface1 at the opacity that will actually ship — no glass effect that disappears on device.
  • Legibility over decoration. Title/artist sit on the scrim at ≥4.5:1 against the darkest cover; long titles ellipsize at two lines, never wrap under the controls.
  • No permission cost. Widgets read the current session only; no new permissions, no background service.
